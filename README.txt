BHS Digital & Business Solutions website
==========================================

Upload all files in this folder to the root of a GitHub Pages repository.

Important before going live:
1. Replace info@bhsdigital.co.uk in all HTML files with your real business email.
2. Replace the example portfolio items with your real work.
3. Update pricing if required.
4. For a real online contact form, connect contact.html to a form service or backend.
5. Then enable GitHub Pages and connect your domain.

Files:
- index.html
- services.html
- pricing.html
- portfolio.html
- about.html
- contact.html
- style.css
- script.js
