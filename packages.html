Yes. Each package states its included revision rounds. New requirements or major changes may be quoted separately.

Is advertising spend included?

No. BHS management fees and the advertising budget paid to Google, Meta or TikTok are separate.

Can I start with one service?

Yes. You can order a single document/design or combine services into a package.

Can you work with my existing website or social accounts?

Yes, where access and the required platform permissions are available.
